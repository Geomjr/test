# Decentralised.co: content extraction and style book

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`style-book.md`](./style-book.md) | **The style book.** It covers voice and tone, explanation craft (how DCo describes and explains), composition, titles, mechanics, sourcing and disclosure, visual design (logo, palette, type, cover template, chart styles), author voices, checklists and fill-in templates. |
| [`catalogue.md`](./catalogue.md) | An index of all 274 posts in the publication's sitemap, with the title, subtitle, date, byline, format and URL where known. Posts marked ✓ were read closely for the style book. |

## Method and limits
- Direct network access to decentralised.co was blocked from this environment. Pages were retrieved through a paid web-fetch service with a limited credit budget. Each fetched page was capped at 30,000 characters.
- **Text:** the sitemap, archive pages, About page and Substack archive API were used for metadata. 18 representative posts (2020–2026, all main authors and formats) were read in full or up to the cap. Podcast episode pages were not read.
- **Images:** the logo, 6 covers and 12 in-article charts were downloaded directly from Substack's image store and analysed for layout, typography and colour.
- **Copyright:** full article texts are **not** mirrored here because they are Decentralised.co's copyrighted work. The style book quotes short excerpts for illustration only. Read the originals at https://www.decentralised.co.
