## The prompt (verbatim, from the application)

> 9. As part of the application, you will need to create a one-page write-up on a thesis or sub-sector from the list below. Categories are intentionally broad to allow room for creativity; however, please feel free to narrow your focus in the write-up (e.g. if you choose Consumer Healthcare as your topic, feel free to write about a sub-sector within to get more specific, such as "Aging in Place" or "Mental Health". Some additional examples can be found below.)

Chosen category: **Fintech / Blockchain**, narrowed to **institutional crypto** and then to one specific sub-thesis (see `02_shortlist_and_scoring.md` and the final one-pager in `../thesis/`).

## What the program is (from public descriptions)

- Two-year, application-only program that prepares CBS students for a VC career.
- Fellows are matched with a New York VC fund mentor and **write an investment thesis** with that fund; they also run diligence for the Lang Fund and get priority enrollment in VC courses.
- Past fellows' theses in the fintech/crypto lane have included payments fintech, blockchain interoperability, and agentic AI in fintech (from the "Meet the Fellows" PDFs). The write-up is therefore read as a *sample of thesis-building ability*: specificity, a clear "why now", a view on where value accrues, and evidence of independent thinking matter more than breadth.

## Constraints set by the applicant

1. **Topic:** institutional crypto (institutions = banks, asset managers, broker-dealers, exchanges, corporates, market infrastructures).
2. **Not anchored in prior employers' business lines.** The applicant's employers can be cited as evidence, but the thesis must not be a rehash of:
   - crypto-collateralised lending / prime-brokerage-style credit to trading firms,
   - crypto ETPs and structured products (staking, covered-call, index products),
   - automated / algorithmic crypto trading,
   - web3 media, events, and incubation.
3. **One page.** Target ~450–600 words of body text, or ~550–650 with a short header and a "companies to watch" line.
4. **Deep research.** Every number and regulatory claim in the one-pager must be dated and sourced (see `../thesis/fact-check.md`).

- Long-term goal: a crypto-focused VC firm; already co-founded a small crypto venture and worked at an early-stage tech VC.
